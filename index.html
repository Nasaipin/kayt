About KayT

An Instrument of Praise & Spiritual Awakening

Kwaku Yeboah KayT (often styled simply as KayT) is a revered Ghanaian gospel recording artist, prolific songwriter, acoustic guitarist, and worship leader known across the nation for his profound, atmospheric live worship encounters.

With a heart dedicated to leading believers into intense spiritual worship, KayT frequently collaborates with New Generation—a vibrant gospel choir network—creating acoustic and choral praise experiences that touch hearts globally.
